# Estimating the Social Cost of Corporate Data Breaches

This repository contains the code accompanying the paper:

> **Estimating the Social Cost of Corporate Data Breaches**  
> Lina Alkarmi, Armin Sarabi, Mingyan Liu  
> University of Michigan  
> *Submitted to Journal of Cybersecurity, 2026*

We bridge two traditionally disconnected data sources — a 13-year longitudinal record of identity theft (IDT) victimization from the NCVS Identity Theft Supplement (ITS) and a comprehensive data breach chronology from the Privacy Rights Clearinghouse (PRC) — to estimate the social cost of corporate data breaches.

---

## Data Sources

This repository contains **code only**. Data must be downloaded separately from the original sources:

- **NCVS-ITS (2008–2021):** Six survey waves available through the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR) at [icpsr.umich.edu](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu). Download the raw TSV files for survey years 2008, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, and 2021 and place them in `data/raw/ITS/`.
- **PRC Breach Chronology:** Available at the Privacy Rights Clearinghouse ([privacyrights.org](https://privacyrights.org)). Place in `data/raw/PRC/`.
- **HHS Breach Reports:** Available at the HHS Office for Civil Rights breach portal ([ocrportal.hhs.gov](https://ocrportal.hhs.gov)). Place in `data/raw/PRC_Supplements/`.
- **Maine & New Hampshire breach filings:** Available from their respective state attorney general offices. Place in `data/raw/PRC_Supplements/`.
- **U.S. Population Data:** Used in the saturation model. Place in `data/raw/external/`.

## Reproducing the Analysis

Run scripts in order from part1 through part6 (parts 7–9 are supplementary robustness checks). From the repo root:

```bash
python scripts/part1/harmonize_its.py
python scripts/part2/calculate_social_cost.py
# ... etc.
```

Each script prints its output paths when it runs. See script descriptions below for details.

---

## Script Descriptions

### Part 1 — ITS Harmonization

**`harmonize_its.py`**  
Harmonizes all six ITS survey waves (2008–2021) into a single unified dataset. Applies variable renaming, code remapping, and weight normalization to ensure comparability across waves. Outputs `its_victims.parquet` (victims only) and `its_full.parquet` (all respondents) to `data/processed/part1/`.

---

### Part 2 — Social Cost and Longitudinal Trends

**`calculate_social_cost.py`**  
Core social cost analysis. Applies the three-component monetization model (out-of-pocket losses, opportunity cost of lost time, and healthcare costs) to produce per-victim and national cost estimates for each survey year, plus demographic breakdowns by age, race/ethnicity, education, and income. Outputs to `data/processed/part2/social_cost/`.

**`social_cost_by_theft_type.py`**  
Calculates the complete social cost profile broken down by IDT type (existing credit card, existing bank account, new account fraud, etc.) using inclusive filtering so victims with multiple types appear in each relevant group. Outputs to `data/processed/part2/`.

**`social_cost_by_theft_type_plotter.py`**  
Generates two publication figures from the theft-type cost analysis: a longitudinal line chart of cost per victim by type, and a relative cost bar chart for 2021. Outputs to `plots/part2/by_theft/`.

**`incident_trends_plotter.py`**  
Primary plotting engine for the paper's longitudinal findings. Produces figures showing shifts in theft type, theft method, discovery method, OOP losses, and social cost evolution across the 13-year study period. Outputs plots 1–9 to `plots/part2/`.

**`social_cost_breach_vs_nonbreach.py`**  
Compares social costs for victims notified that their data was breached versus those who were not, and for victims whose SSN was exposed versus those whose was not. Outputs to `data/processed/part2/social_cost_breach_vs_nonbreach/`.

**`demographics_table.py`**  
Generates a demographic summary table for ITS victims. Outputs `demographic_summary.csv` to `data/processed/part2/`.

**`theft_type_analysis.py`**  
Creates a longitudinal incident characteristic summary across survey waves. Outputs `combined_theft_analysis_by_year.csv` to `data/processed/part2/`.

**`educational_profile_comparison.py`**  
Calculates weighted proportions for victim groups segmented by educational attainment to identify over- and under-representation patterns. Outputs to `data/processed/part2/`.

**`race_profile_comparison.py`**  
Conducts a weighted comparative profile analysis of incident characteristics across racial groups. Outputs to `data/processed/part2/`.

**`hypothesis.py`**  
Statistical tests validating hypotheses about demographic over- and under-representation in the ITS data. Prints results to stdout.

**`census_api/race_table.py`**  
Extracts census race data via the Census API for comparison against ITS demographics. Outputs `census_race_data_2008-2021.csv` to `data/processed/part2/census/`.

---

### Part 3 — PRC Breach Data Cleaning and Augmentation

**`load_raw_PRC_data.py`**  
Deduplicates the PRC breach chronology: where multiple rows share the same `group_uuid` (representing a single breach reported across multiple registries), retains the record with the highest affected count. Outputs `clean_prc_base.csv` to `data/raw/PRC/loaded/`.

**`PRC_fill_in_health.py`**  
Uses official HHS breach reports to fill in missing record counts for healthcare-related incidents in the PRC database. Outputs `prc_hhs_integrated.csv` to `data/processed/part3/`.

**`PRC_Maine_NH_regression.py`**  
Implements the statistical methodology to estimate national breach magnitudes from state-level filings. Matches Maine and New Hampshire state breach filings to PRC national records using fuzzy matching and derives a scaling multiplier (Beta). Produces the final augmented PRC dataset. Outputs `national_scaling_regression_results.csv` and `PRC_augmented.csv` to `data/processed/part3/`.

**`ME_NH_cross_validation.py`**  
Cross-validates the Maine/New Hampshire scaling regression by holding out each state in turn and checking whether the regression estimated from the other generalizes. Outputs validation plots to `plots/part3/`.

**`find_nu.py`**  
Calculates the annual baseline for undisclosed records in the PRC database (nu_u), ensuring zero-count breaches contribute to the cumulative exposure pool. Outputs to `data/processed/part3/`.

---

### Part 4 — Dynamic Saturation Model

**`apply_saturation_model.py`**  
Converts raw breach record counts into "Newly Unique Records" by modeling population saturation and identity density over time (2008–2021). Accounts for the finite size of the U.S. population and the likelihood that any individual's data has already been exposed in prior breaches. Outputs `PRC_Data_After_Saturation_Model.csv` to `data/processed/part4/`.

**`apply_saturation_model_exp.py`** / **`apply_saturation_model_with_decay.py`**  
Variant implementations using exponential decay and time-decayed exposure weighting. Used for sensitivity comparisons.

---

### Part 5 — Breach-to-Victim Conversion Rate and Mega-Breach Costs

**`monthly_data_breach_conversion_beta.py`**  
Primary conversion rate script. Computes the discounted cumulative record pool D_t using a monthly discount factor (α = 0.8) and fits a log-quadratic regression to the resulting breach-to-victim conversion rate. Produces the overlay and conversion rate figures used in the paper. Outputs `conversion_data.csv` to `data/processed/part5/` and plots to `plots/part5/`.

**`monthly_data_breach_conversion.py`** / **`monthly_data_breach_conversion_raw.py`** / **`monthly_data_breach_conversion_lag.py`**  
Variant implementations exploring different conversion rate formulations (no discount, raw records, lagged exposure).

**`calculate_megabreach_cost_extended.py`**  
Extended mega-breach social cost analysis. Computes lower- and upper-bound social cost estimates for all qualifying mega-breaches (≥10M records) using the Wilcoxon-derived lower bound and the log-quadratic lifecycle model upper bound. Includes a `dark_web_verified` flag to distinguish criminal-market breaches from suspected state-sponsored incidents. Outputs to `data/processed/part5/`.

**`calculate_megabreach_cost.py`**  
Earlier version computing costs for Target and Equifax only. Superseded by `calculate_megabreach_cost_extended.py`.

**`breach_type_conversion.py`**  
Computes breach-type-specific conversion rates (HACK, PHYS/PORT, DISC, INSD) by restricting the discounted record pool to each breach mechanism. Produces the breach-type figure from the extensions section. Outputs to `data/processed/part5/` and `plots/part5/`.

**`sensitivity_alpha.py`**  
Sensitivity analysis on the discount factor α ∈ {0.70, 0.80, 0.90}. Refits the log-quadratic model for each value and recomputes upper-bound social costs for all four case study breaches. Outputs `sensitivity_alpha.csv` to `data/processed/part5/`.

**`peak_analysis.py`**  
Identifies and characterizes local peaks in the breach-to-victim conversion rate, investigating their correspondence with ITS survey waves versus actual breach events.

---

### Part 6 — Wilcoxon Tests and Causal Inference

**`wilcoxon_mega_breach_test.py`**  
Runs a longitudinal Wilcoxon signed-rank test sweeping over discovery lags (0–8 months) to test whether mega-breach events cause a statistically significant increase in IDT victim reports. Tests both raw PRC data and saturation-model data. Produces the Wilcoxon figure used in the paper. Outputs plots to `plots/part6/`.

**`wilcoxon_placebo_test.py`**  
Placebo version of the Wilcoxon test. Applies the identical longitudinal sweep to non-mega-breach months to verify the test does not spuriously flag noise as significant. Outputs placebo plots to `plots/part6/`.

**`ccf_breach_idt.py`**  
Computes the cross-correlation function (CCF) between the breach record exposure series and the IDT victim series after AR pre-whitening, characterizing the lag structure between breach events and victim reports. Outputs CCF figure to `plots/part6/`.

**`pooled_its_mega_breach.py`**  
Pooled interrupted time series (ITS) regression across all mega-breach events. Estimates a panel fixed-effects model with pre/post indicators as a complementary causal inference check alongside the Wilcoxon test. Outputs regression results and figure to `plots/part6/`.

---

### Part 7 — Regression Analysis

**`breach_victim_regression.py`**  
Regresses monthly IDT victim counts on breach exposure metrics across mega-breach windows, with configurable lag and optional exclusion of Yahoo. Outputs regression summary and plots to `plots/part7/`.

---

### Part 8 — Robustness Checks

**`blackout_check.py`**  
Investigates whether ITS survey blackout periods (gaps between waves during which victim counts are interpolated) confound the Wilcoxon test results. Reconstructs pre-interpolation monthly data and checks whether mega-breach effects survive the exclusion of interpolated months. Outputs to `plots/part8/`.

---

### Part 9 — Validated Subset Analysis

**`valid_subset_analysis.py`**  
Restricts the Wilcoxon analysis to a high-confidence subset of mega-breaches where ITS survey data (rather than interpolated estimates) exists in the post-breach window, providing a conservative robustness check. Outputs to `plots/part9/`.
